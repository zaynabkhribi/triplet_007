# m1: real fire and a fake jam at the same time

Zone: A (traffic) and B (building)
Layers involved: Twin, Guardian, Brain

## Trigger

* From the page: open the Scenario Control page of the Twin (port 8080) and click the card "Real fire and a fake jam at the same time".
* From the command line, at the root of the repository: `python scenarios/m1.py`.

Each run writes one log file in `logs/`, named `m1-<date>-<time>.log`.

## What the Twin does

* +5 s: the building starts to burn (smoke and temperature rise) and `us-a-traf-03` starts to report a constant fake jam.
* +20 s: the alert for the fire is CRITICAL. In the timed run the Twin plays the Brain's answer to the fire at this point.
* +45 s: the fire is out and the fire alert clears. The fake jam stays until Reset all, because a sensor that lies does not stop by itself.

## What the other layers should do

The Guardian marks the building TRUSTED (smoke and temperature rise together) and `us-a-traf-03` UNTRUSTED (constant values). The Brain answers the fire with the alarm and the dispatch, and ignores the fake jam: no command on the lights or the sign.

## Pass criteria

* The log has COMMAND lines for the alarm and the dispatch, and none for the lights or the sign.
* The fire alert is CLEARED.
* The Guardian verdict for `us-a-traf-03` is UNTRUSTED.
